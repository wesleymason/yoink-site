White elephant gift ideas

Gifts people fight over, by budget, plus the ones to leave at the store.

The best white elephant gifts get stolen. That means something people actually want, or something so funny the room fights over it. Here are ideas by budget, plus what to skip.

What makes a gift steal-worthy

  • Useful to almost anyone. Good coffee, a nice candle, a cozy blanket. Broad appeal beats niche every time.
  • Funny on sight. The joke has to land the second it's unwrapped, not after an explanation.
  • Slightly over the budget in feel. Something that looks like a splurge, even if it wasn't.
  • Easy to carry home. Nobody wants to steal a 20-pound thing.

Under $15

  • A bag of properly good coffee beans or a tin of nice tea
  • Fuzzy socks, the thick kind
  • A scratch-off lottery ticket taped to a candy bar
  • A hot sauce sampler
  • A deck of cards with a card game people haven't played
  • A small cast iron skillet
  • A puzzle with a ridiculous picture
  • Fancy chocolate, the kind in a nice box

$20 to $30

  • An insulated tumbler or travel mug
  • A board game that plays in 20 minutes
  • A plush throw blanket
  • A mini waffle maker
  • Bluetooth speaker, the small rugged kind
  • A bottle of something good, if everyone's an adult
  • A smart plug or a set of string lights
  • A nice set of kitchen knives, just one great chef's knife

$40 to $50

  • Wireless earbuds
  • An electric kettle with temperature control
  • A weighted eye mask and a silk pillowcase
  • A portable phone charger with real capacity
  • A good cocktail kit with a shaker and jigger
  • A Lego set made for adults

Funny gifts that still get stolen

  • A giant bag of gummy bears, the 5-pound one
  • A toilet night light
  • A mug with a photo of the host on it
  • Socks printed with the host's face, same idea
  • An absurdly large inflatable anything
  • A "world's okayest" trophy
  • A paint-by-numbers of a famous painting
  • A Pez dispenser collection

Gifts to skip

  • Regifted junk. The vase you hated is the vase everyone hates.
  • Anything that needs sizing. Clothes, rings, shoes.
  • Inside jokes. Half the room won't get it.
  • Gift cards to one store. Fine, but boring; nobody steals a gift card.
  • Anything mean. Diet books, "as seen on TV" fixes for real problems.

Themes that make shopping easier

If your group needs help, pick a theme: everything from a gas station, everything under $10, best thing from your kitchen, or "the gift you'd want to steal." A theme narrows the choice and makes the pile funnier.
