Yankee swap vs white elephant

Same game, different names, and the house rules that tend to come with each.

They're the same game. White elephant, Yankee swap and Dirty Santa are regional names for one gift exchange: open a gift or steal one. The real differences are in the house rules each name tends to carry.

Same game, three names

NameWhere you hear itWhat people usually mean
White elephantMost of the USFunny or useless gifts, steals, a steal limit
Yankee swapNew EnglandNicer gifts people actually want, with a swap at the end
Dirty SantaThe SouthSame as white elephant, with more stealing and more trash talk

Where the name "white elephant" comes from

The story goes that kings of Siam gave rare white elephants to courtiers they wanted to ruin: the animal was sacred, couldn't be put to work, and cost a fortune to keep. A "white elephant" became anything expensive to own and impossible to get rid of, which describes a lot of gag gifts.

The differences that actually matter

The gifts

A Yankee swap usually means decent gifts within a budget, things you'd be glad to take home. White elephant and Dirty Santa lean toward gag gifts, though plenty of groups mix both. Say which you want on the invite; it's the single biggest source of mismatched gifts.

The swap at the end

In a Yankee swap, the player who went first traditionally gets a final swap, trading their gift for any other once everything is open. Some white elephant groups do this too, but it's less universal. Decide in advance.

Steal limits

White elephant groups usually freeze a gift after three steals. Yankee swap often allows more stealing, since the gifts are worth fighting over, and leans on the final swap to end it. Dirty Santa is the most steal-heavy, which is where the name comes from.

Who's playing

Yankee swap tends to be family and office parties. Dirty Santa is friends who enjoy a bit of ruthlessness. White elephant is both.

Which should you host?

Pick the gifts first, then the name. Want people to leave with something good? Call it a Yankee swap, set a $25 to $30 budget, and allow a final swap. Want a loud, silly night? Call it white elephant or Dirty Santa, set a $15 to $20 budget, tell people funny is the goal, and freeze gifts after three steals. The rules are otherwise identical, and so is how you run it.
