How do I put the party on my TV?

Unlock TV mode, then open Control Center on your iPhone, tap Screen Mirroring and choose your Apple TV or AirPlay-compatible TV. Yoink shows a TV-sized view of the party while your phone keeps the controls. A Lightning or USB-C to HDMI adapter works too.

My TV keeps switching back to another app

On an Apple TV, quit any apps that are playing video before you start mirroring. Some streaming apps take the screen back after a few seconds. Then start Screen Mirroring again from your iPhone.

How do I restore my TV mode purchase?

Open Yoink, tap the gear on the home screen, then tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it.

Is Yoink free?

Yes. Running parties and every duel game are free. TV mode is a one-time $4.99 unlock, with no subscription.

Can I change the rules mid-party?

You set steal limits, duels and the final swap before shuffling. Which duel games are in the mix can be changed any time from the party's game list.

Where is my party data stored?

Only on your iPhone. Yoink has no accounts and doesn't upload anything. See the Privacy Policy for details.
