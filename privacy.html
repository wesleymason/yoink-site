Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

The short version: Yoink has no accounts and shows no ads. A party you run by yourself stays on your device. When you invite guests, the RSVP names they choose go to you through iCloud, and once the party starts, the game itself (player names, who holds which gift, and any gift photos) is shared through iCloud with the guests who have the invite, so they can follow along on their phones. Separately, anonymous app-usage signals go to Meta to measure our App Store ads, described under Ad measurement. We never see any of it.

Who we are

Yoink: White Elephant Games ("Yoink", "we", "us") is an iPhone app for running white elephant gift exchanges. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app and this website.

Information we collect

Yoink does not ask you to create an account and does not send personal information to us. Three kinds of information can leave your device, none of them to us: an RSVP, which goes to the party's host; the live game of a party with invited guests, which goes to those guests; and ad-measurement signals, which go to Meta. Each is described below.

RSVPs

When a guest taps "I'll be there" on a party invite, Yoink asks for a name and stores it, along with the party's invite code and whether they're coming, in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit). The host's copy of Yoink reads those RSVPs to fill in the player list. The name is whatever the guest types, and it's the only thing shared. Guests can withdraw at any time from the invite, and the host can edit or remove names on their player list. We don't see RSVPs, use them for anything else, or keep them after the party. RSVPing requires being signed in to iCloud; Apple's handling of iCloud data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Following along

When a host starts a party that guests were invited to, Yoink shares the game through Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) so those guests can follow it live on their own phones: the player names and turn order, each move (who opened, stole or won what), whose turn it is, and the recap at the end. Gift photos and names that the host or a guest adds are shared the same way. Guests who RSVP'd can also get a notification when the party starts, when it's their turn, when someone takes their gift, and when the game is over; a guest who didn't RSVP can tap their name on the board to get those notifications too.

This information is keyed to the party's invite code and is only readable by someone who has the invite link. It is not public, not searchable, and not sent to us; it travels between the host's and guests' phones through iCloud under Apple's terms. Parties started with "Start a party" (no invites) share nothing. Hosts can remove a shared party at any time by discarding it, which also tells guests it's cancelled, and guests can remove a party they followed from their own phone. RSVPing or following along requires being signed in to iCloud; Apple's handling of iCloud data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Information stored on your device

To run a party, Yoink saves what you type and choose in the app, such as:

  • Party names, player names and the turn order
  • Gifts, steals, duel results and party recaps
  • Custom duel games you create
  • Your settings, like sound, haptics and appearance

This information is stored on your iPhone. For a party with invited guests, the game is also shared with them as described under Following along; otherwise it never leaves your device. It is never sent to us, and we have no way to see it. Deleting a party removes it, and deleting the app removes everything Yoink has stored on the phone.

Camera and photos

You can add a photo to a gift so everyone remembers which is which. Yoink asks for permission before it uses your camera or photo library, and only uses them when you choose to add a gift photo. Photos are saved with the party on your device. In a party with invited guests, a gift photo is also shared through iCloud with the host and the guests following along, as described above; in a party without invites it is not uploaded anywhere.

TV mode

TV mode shows the party on a TV using AirPlay or a cable. The picture goes straight from your iPhone to your TV over your local connection. Nothing is sent to us.

Purchases

The TV mode unlock is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple ID, email or payment details. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Ad measurement

We advertise Yoink on Facebook and Instagram. So that Meta can tell us whether those ads led to installs, the app includes Meta's software development kit, which reports to Meta when the app is opened, when it goes to the background, and when the TV mode purchase is made, along with device information such as the iPhone model, iOS version, app version, language and time zone, and a random identifier Apple assigns to this app on your device. Meta may also receive crash and performance reports from the kit.

Yoink never asks permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, so the advertising identifier is not shared with Meta and these signals are not used to build a profile of you or to show you personalized ads. Apple's privacy-preserving ad attribution (SKAdNetwork) may separately tell Meta, without identifying you, that an install came from one of our ads. Meta's use of the information it receives is described in Meta's Privacy Policy; the data it collects is also listed in Yoink's App Privacy details on the App Store.

Crash reports

If you've turned on "Share With App Developers" in your iPhone's privacy settings, Apple may share anonymous crash and usage reports with us to help fix problems. These reports don't identify you, and you can turn sharing off at any time in Settings.

Sharing

We don't sell or rent personal information. Party invites and recaps go only where you choose to send them. An RSVP name is shared with the host of that party and, once the party starts, with the other guests following along, since it appears on the shared board. The live game and gift photos are shared only with people who have that party's invite link. Ad-measurement signals are shared with Meta as described above, and with no one else.

Children

Yoink is made for families and all ages. The app has no accounts and collects no personal information beyond the names and gift photos a host or guest chooses to add to a party, which are shared only with the people invited to that party.

This website

This site has no accounts or analytics of its own. It does carry the Pinterest Tag, which Pinterest uses to measure whether people who saw a Yoink pin or ad visited the site, and may set a Pinterest cookie in your browser to do so; see Pinterest's Privacy Policy, and you can opt out of personalized ads in your Pinterest settings. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security, as described in GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version here and update the effective date above.
